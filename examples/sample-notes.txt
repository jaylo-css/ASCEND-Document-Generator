Event: Arduino Basics Workshop 2026
Date: October 10, 2026, 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM
Venue: Room 401, NU Fairview
The workshop introduced 45 first-year CpE students to breadboarding and Arduino programming. Engr. Maria Santos was the resource speaker. Participants built a blinking LED and a traffic light circuit.
